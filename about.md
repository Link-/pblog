I am a senior software engineer at GitHub, focused on building reliable systems and making the work around them more effective.

My career has moved through internet-scale infrastructure, developer platforms, engineering leadership, and technical advisory work. The common thread is a fascination with how people and systems behave under pressure—and how thoughtful design can make both better.

This blog is a collection of notes from experience. Some are brief observations; others are longer technical deep dives into teams, learning, decision-making, and the craft of software engineering.
