Bassem Dghaidi is a senior software engineer at GitHub who enjoys solving hard problems in internet-scale distributed systems and removing organisational friction.

Across more than 15 years in technology, he has worked with startups, global enterprises, and Fortune 500 companies across developer tools, FinTech, transportation, logistics, and education. His talks turn those experiences into practical stories about engineering, leadership, and the changing shape of software development.

Bassem is also a LinkedIn Top Voice, course author, podcaster, and active content creator focused on empowering the next generation of technologists.
